Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do airport pickups work with Jano Limo at O'Hare & Midway?

We continuously monitor your flight arrival time in real time. Once your plane lands, your chauffeur texts you directly. For curbside pickup, your vehicle approaches immediately upon your exit. Meet & Greet inside terminal baggage claim is also available upon request.

What happens if my flight to Chicago is delayed?

No stress! Because we track your flight number automatically, your pickup time adjusts seamlessly. There are no surprise wait-time fees for flight delays beyond your control.

What is your hourly service minimum?

Our hourly charter service requires a 3-hour minimum booking. This guarantees a dedicated vehicle and chauffeur on standby for your custom multi-stop itinerary.

Are child seats or extra luggage options available?

Yes! Infant, toddler, and booster seats can be requested in the Special Instructions section when booking. Our Cadillac XT6 and Escalade ESV feature spacious luggage space.
